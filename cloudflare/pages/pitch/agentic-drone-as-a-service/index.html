Model assumptions (all illustrative and unvalidated)

  • Currency & FX: S$, at US$1 = S$1.30.
  • Dock + drone: US$26,412 (≈S$34.3k) leased over 36 months at 8% APR = S$1,076/month.
  • Labour rates: pilot-in-command S$60/h; manual warehouse labour S$25/h.
  • Missions: 20 per site per month. Churn: 2% per month, with a 36-month LTV horizon.
  • Team: loaded cost S$8.5k per FTE per month.
  • Recognised revenue: mean of opening and closing MRR × 12, plus onboarding fees (S$1,500 per new site) and dossiers (60 / 300 / 800 per year).
  • Gross margin: the tier mix alone gives ≈58%. Year 1 is set to 55% for ramp inefficiency, then rises through the automation levers on slide 8.
  • Hardware: full-service hardware is owned by the lessor; we carry no capex.
  • Year-3 exit run-rate: exit MRR × 64% gross margin, minus average monthly Year-3 opex (S$326K).
